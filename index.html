From France

Direct train to Angers

Direct TGVs run from Paris-Montparnasse to Angers Saint-Laud in as little as 1 hour 20 minutes; direct Nantes–Angers trains take about 40 minutes.

Check SNCF routes ↗

From Spain

Fly via Nantes, then train

If a direct flight is available, fly to Nantes Atlantique. The airport shuttle reaches Nantes station in about 20 minutes; direct trains to Angers take roughly 40 minutes.

Rail travel from Barcelona and other Spanish cities is possible via the French high-speed network; allow a full day.

Nantes—Angers trains ↗

Registration

Registration is open. One form per participant.

To correct an existing registration, email the organisers.

Please do not include sensitive personal or medical information.

Nothing is published automatically. With consent, only your name and affiliation may appear in the participant list.

Cancel a registration

Enter the name and email used to register. The organisers will email that address for verification.

After verification, the organisers will remove your registration and any public participant entry.
