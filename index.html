01 The meeting

About the conference

Singularities, viewed from both sides of the Pyrenees.

The Spain–France Conference on Singularities and Applications will bring researchers together for five days of mathematics at Université d’Angers.

The conference centres on a direct exchange between the Spanish and French singularity communities, with Angers as their meeting point.

Talks and discussions will move between the theory of singularities and the places where it is used. The scientific scope, speakers and participation details will be published as they are confirmed.
