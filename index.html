Singularities, viewed from both sides of the Pyrenees.

The Spain–France Conference on Singularities and Applications will bring researchers together for five days of mathematics at Université d’Angers.

The conference centres on a direct exchange between the Spanish and French singularity communities, with Angers as their meeting point.

Talks and discussions will move between the theory of singularities and the places where it is used. The scientific scope, speakers and participation details will be published as they are confirmed.

From France

Recommended

Direct train to Angers

From Paris, take a direct TGV from Montparnasse to Angers Saint-Laud. The fastest services take about 1 hour 20 minutes. From Nantes, direct trains take about 40 minutes.

Check SNCF routes ↗

From Spain

Most practical

Fly via Nantes, then train

Where a direct flight is available, arrive at Nantes Atlantique. The airport shuttle reaches Nantes station in about 20 minutes; direct trains then reach Angers in roughly 40 minutes.

All-rail journeys from Barcelona and other Spanish cities are also possible through the French high-speed network; allow a full travel day.
